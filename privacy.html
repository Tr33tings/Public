Privacy Policy

Hermes is a personal automation assistant running on the owner's own home server. It accesses the owner's Google account (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts) only with the owner's explicit consent and only to perform tasks the owner requests.

Data is processed locally and is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No user data is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs. Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions page.
